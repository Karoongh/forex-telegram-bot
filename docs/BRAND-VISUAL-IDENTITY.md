# Forex Trader Visual Identity & Image Generation Guidelines

> Living visual-brand specification for the fictional recurring trader character used across the Forex project.
>
> This document is the single source of truth for the character's visual identity, photography rules, lifestyle representation, lighting, shadows, natural behavior, and reusable image-generation prompts.

## 1. Purpose

The project needs a consistent fictional trader persona for social media, educational content, market-analysis posts, Telegram content, and other brand-facing materials.

The goal is **not** to create a stereotypical "forex millionaire" image.

The intended perception is:

> Successful, knowledgeable, disciplined, financially comfortable, and trustworthy — while still looking like a normal real person.

The visual identity must support credibility through consistency, professionalism, realism, transparency, and relatable behavior rather than exaggerated displays of wealth.

### Important distinction

Persona decisions in this document are **brand-design choices**, not factual claims that a particular gender, age, ethnicity, location, body type, or appearance objectively makes a financial professional more trustworthy.

Trust should ultimately be supported by the service's real behavior, transparency, risk disclosure, performance reporting, security practices, and customer experience.

## 2. Character Identity

### Character ID

**FX-TRADER-01**

This identifier should be used internally whenever a recurring character is generated.

### Core appearance

- Fictional male
- Approximately 32 years old
- Middle Eastern / Mediterranean appearance
- Light olive skin
- Dark brown eyes
- Thick dark brown hair
- Naturally styled hair with a subtle side part
- Short, well-groomed dark beard and mustache
- Natural eyebrows
- Straight, medium-sized nose
- Defined but natural jawline
- Realistic facial asymmetry
- Realistic skin texture and pores
- Approximately 180 cm
- Healthy, moderately athletic build
- Naturally broad shoulders
- Normal human proportions

He should look like a successful professional in his early 30s, **not like a fashion model or fitness influencer**.

### Identity consistency

Future images must preserve:

- Same recognizable face
- Same approximate age
- Same hairstyle
- Same beard style
- Same skin tone
- Same eye color
- Same body proportions
- Same overall physical identity

A reference image should be maintained and supplied to image-generation tools whenever the tool supports character references, image references, seeds, LoRA/identity conditioning, or equivalent mechanisms.

## 6. Natural Human Behavior

The character must **not always pose for the camera**.

The images should often feel like real moments from his daily life.

### Example scenes

1. Sitting comfortably on a sofa with his legs stretched out while working on a laptop.
2. Sitting at his trading desk while analyzing forex charts.
3. Looking at a laptop instead of the camera.
4. Drinking coffee while reviewing market data.
5. Working on a balcony in the morning.
6. Walking through a modern city while checking his phone.
7. Reading a financial book on a sofa.
8. Taking notes beside his laptop.
9. Working casually in a café.
10. Standing beside a window while thinking.
11. Recording a natural educational video.
12. Leaning back after finishing a trading session.
13. Looking away from the camera while concentrating.
14. Laughing naturally during a casual moment.
15. Working late at night using normal room lighting.

### Pose rules

Do not repeatedly use:

- Perfectly straight posture
- Symmetrical poses
- Hands in identical positions
- Centered portraits
- Direct eye contact
- Forced smiles

Direct eye contact is allowed, but it should be only one of several recurring visual behaviors.

The character should sometimes be photographed:

- From the side
- From behind
- Over the shoulder
- In profile
- Looking at a screen
- Walking
- Relaxing
- Reading
- Working
- Thinking

The viewer should feel that the camera **captured a moment**, rather than that the character stopped to pose for an advertisement.

## 7. Photography Philosophy

The photographs should resemble authentic real-world photography.

### Required characteristics

- Photorealistic
- Documentary/editorial feeling
- Natural perspective
- Realistic lens behavior
- Realistic depth of field
- Natural framing
- Slight technical imperfections are acceptable

Preferred lenses:

- 35mm
- 50mm
- 85mm equivalent

Different focal lengths may be selected according to the scene.

### Deliberate imperfection

Images do not need to be technically perfect.

Acceptable:

- Slight lens softness
- Mild motion blur
- Subtle sensor noise
- Realistic high-ISO grain
- Slight exposure variation
- Minor framing imperfections
- Occasional imperfect focus

Avoid making every image look like a high-budget commercial campaign.

## 8. Lighting Rules

### Natural light is the default

Preferred sources:

- Window daylight
- Cloudy daylight
- Morning light
- Late-afternoon light
- Ordinary room lighting
- Practical desk lamps
- Ceiling lights
- Natural mixed lighting

Avoid defaulting to:

- Studio three-point lighting
- Perfect key/fill/rim setups
- Artificial cinematic lighting
- Strong commercial portrait lighting
- Excessive rim lights
- Perfectly controlled highlights

The scene should look as though it was photographed using the light that naturally existed in that environment.

---

## 9. Shadow Rules — Critical

Shadows are one of the most important realism constraints.

Every visible shadow must be physically consistent with the actual light sources.

Shadows should:

- Fall in physically plausible directions
- Have realistic softness
- Become softer with larger light sources
- Interact correctly with furniture and objects
- Appear naturally on the face
- Appear beneath furniture
- Appear on the floor
- Include believable contact shadows
- Match the position of windows and lamps
- Respect occlusion between objects

Never create:

- Floating shadows
- Duplicate shadows
- Shadows pointing in contradictory directions
- Dark patches without a physical cause
- Perfectly symmetrical shadows
- Unrealistic object-to-floor relationships
- Objects illuminated from impossible directions

Some parts of a room should naturally remain darker.

Do not force every object to receive equal illumination.

The objective is **physical plausibility**, not visual perfection.

Use this as the stable base prompt across image-generation tools.

~~~text
Create a photorealistic, natural-looking fictional male forex trader character for a professional financial brand.

CHARACTER IDENTITY:
FX-TRADER-01, a fictional 32-year-old Middle Eastern / Mediterranean man with light olive skin, dark brown eyes, thick dark brown naturally styled hair with a subtle side part, short well-groomed dark beard and mustache, natural eyebrows, straight medium-sized nose, defined but natural jawline, realistic facial asymmetry and realistic skin texture.

Approximately 180 cm tall, healthy moderately athletic build, naturally broad shoulders and realistic human proportions.

He should look like a successful professional in his early 30s, not a fashion model, fitness influencer or celebrity.

PERSONALITY:
Intelligent, disciplined, calm, experienced, approachable, financially responsible, stable and professional.

He must NOT look like a get-rich-quick guru.

LIFESTYLE:
Successful but relatable. Financially comfortable but not excessively wealthy.

ENVIRONMENT:
A believable modern apartment, home office, living room, sofa, café, coworking space, balcony, hotel room or realistic city environment.

The environment may be stylish and premium but must feel lived-in and authentic.

Include realistic personal details such as books, coffee, laptop charger, phone, headphones, notebook, plants and natural desk organization.

CLOTHING:
Minimal sophisticated everyday professional clothing such as a dark charcoal crew-neck shirt, black or navy polo, white or light-blue shirt, dark trousers, clean minimal shoes and a simple understated wristwatch.

No visible logos and no flashy jewelry.

BEHAVIOR:
Do not always pose for the camera.

The character should often behave naturally as if photographed during a real moment of his daily life.

He may be working on a laptop, analyzing forex charts, drinking coffee, reading, taking notes, walking, sitting on a sofa with his legs naturally stretched out, working at a desk, relaxing, looking through a window or talking naturally.

He should frequently look at what he is doing instead of looking at the camera.

Natural asymmetry, spontaneous posture and varied body language are required.

PHOTOGRAPHY:
Authentic real-world documentary/editorial photography.
35mm, 50mm or 85mm equivalent lens depending on the scene.
Natural perspective.
Realistic depth of field.
Natural framing.
Subtle imperfections are acceptable.

LIGHTING:
Use realistic available light such as natural window daylight, cloudy daylight, morning light, late-afternoon light, ordinary room lighting, ceiling lights or practical desk lamps.

Avoid studio-style lighting.

Avoid perfect three-point lighting, artificial rim lighting, dramatic commercial lighting and excessive cinematic effects.

SHADOWS:
Shadows must be physically consistent with the actual light sources.

Natural direction, realistic softness, realistic contact shadows, realistic occlusion and believable interaction with furniture and objects.

No floating shadows, duplicated shadows, contradictory shadow directions or unexplained dark patches.

Some parts of the scene may naturally remain darker.

SKIN:
Realistic pores, beard texture, individual hair strands, subtle facial imperfections, natural asymmetry and realistic skin texture.

No plastic skin, beauty retouching or AI-perfect face.

OVERALL FEEL:
The photograph should look like a real moment from a real trader's life.

It should NOT look like an AI-generated advertisement for a forex guru.

Successful but relatable.
Professional but human.
Confident but not arrogant.
Attractive but not model-perfect.
Comfortable but not excessively wealthy.
Technical but approachable.
~~~

## 14. Scene Prompt Template

The Master Prompt should remain stable.

Only the scene should normally change.

Use:

~~~text
SCENE:

FX-TRADER-01 is [describe the specific activity].

LOCATION:

[describe the realistic location].

TIME:

[time of day / weather / session].

BODY LANGUAGE:

[describe natural posture and behavior].

CAMERA:

[wide / medium / close-up / over-the-shoulder / side profile].

LIGHT:

[describe the actual available light source].

IMPORTANT:

The image should feel like an authentic candid moment captured during everyday life, not a staged advertisement.
~~~

### Example: Sofa

~~~text
SCENE:

FX-TRADER-01 is sitting comfortably on a modern fabric sofa with his legs naturally stretched out. A laptop is resting on his thighs and he is reviewing forex charts.

LOCATION:

A comfortable modern apartment living room with realistic furniture, books and a small coffee table.

TIME:

Early morning.

BODY LANGUAGE:

Relaxed posture, focused on the laptop screen, not looking at the camera. One hand rests naturally near the laptop while the other holds a coffee cup.

CAMERA:

Candid medium-wide photograph from slightly to the side.

LIGHT:

Soft natural morning light entering through a nearby window.

IMPORTANT:

Natural shadows, realistic contact shadows, realistic clothing wrinkles and authentic available-light photography. Do not make the scene look staged.
~~~

### Example: Trading Desk

~~~text
SCENE:

FX-TRADER-01 is sitting at his trading desk during a normal working session, leaning slightly forward while studying forex charts on one monitor.

LOCATION:

A realistic home office with two or three professional monitors, laptop, notebook, coffee and normal personal belongings.

TIME:

Late afternoon.

BODY LANGUAGE:

Focused on the screen, not looking at the camera.

CAMERA:

Three-quarter environmental portrait.

LIGHT:

Natural daylight from the side combined with a normal desk lamp.

IMPORTANT:

Physically realistic light and shadows. No studio lighting. No exaggerated cinematic effects. The photograph should look like a real moment during his workday.
~~~

## 16. Character Consistency Workflow

When the image-generation platform supports it:

1. Create one high-quality reference image for FX-TRADER-01.
2. Store the reference in the project's visual assets.
3. Use the reference image for future generations.
4. Keep the character description unchanged.
5. Change only the scene, activity, location, clothing variation, camera angle or time of day.
6. Avoid regenerating the identity from scratch when consistency matters.
7. Review face, hairstyle, beard, skin tone and body proportions before publishing.
8. Reject images that substantially change the character's identity.

The project should treat the reference image as the visual identity anchor.

## 17. Brand Credibility Rules

Images must never be the sole basis for claims of trading performance or financial reliability.

Visual content should be supported by actual:

- Risk disclosures
- Transparent performance information
- Clear service terms
- Security practices
- Customer support
- Realistic expectations
- Accurate market information

Avoid visual communication that implies:

- Guaranteed profit
- Guaranteed returns
- Zero risk
- Effortless wealth
- Certain market predictions
- Guaranteed success

The character is a **brand persona**, not evidence of investment performance.
